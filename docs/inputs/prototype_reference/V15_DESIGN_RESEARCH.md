# House Dark V15 — award-site design research translated into product behaviour

## The pattern across the strongest references

The useful lesson is not "use more WebGL". The strongest sites choose a physical or editorial metaphor and make the entire experience obey it.

### Siena Film Foundation
- Homepage/archive behave like a filmstrip rather than a normal content grid.
- Scroll affects blur, saturation and perceived depth.
- Navigation adapts to the current content layer.
- House Dark translation: one persistent vertical House axis moves through every chapter; Library becomes a draggable typographic archive; motion speed can subtly affect optical softness.

### Seasoned by Koto
- The object determines the interaction model. The site scrolls vertically, then books switch to a horizontal reading flow.
- House Dark translation: Library becomes a horizontal archive because records are something you move through, while Tonight remains a focused vertical ritual.

### Mouthful of Dust
- Direct manipulation and guided cinematic movement coexist.
- Users can click/drag or allow the experience to carry them.
- House Dark translation: direct manipulation for the archive and sealed objects, while Tonight remains choreographed and controlled.

### PP Fragment
- Typography is the content and the scenery.
- House Dark translation: themes and reviews are spatial material rather than chips, cards or rows.

### Cartier Watches & Wonders 2025
- Premium comes from controlled transformation between distinct worlds, not from generic luxury styling.
- House Dark translation: each major chapter gets a material world from the existing palette while the HD axis keeps them related.

## V15 design thesis

**House Dark should feel like one physical House changing rooms.**

The HD monogram is no longer a repeated logo. Its shared vertical spine becomes the persistent spatial axis of the product.

- Entry: the mark is discovered by light.
- Tonight: the axis sits beside the sealed programme object.
- Trust Us: the axis moves to the centre and becomes the gravity point for floating themes.
- The Room: the axis moves left while other voices travel through depth.
- Circle: the axis becomes the dividing seam between private receipt and sending.
- Library: the axis becomes an archive registration line on Programme Paper.

## Material chapters

- Entry / Tonight: Projection Black + Programme Paper object
- Trust Us: Bottle Green
- The Room: Projection Black
- Circle: House Black + Oxblood
- Library: Programme Paper + Tobacco / Oxblood registration
- Patina Brass: timing, rules, active state only

## Interaction changes in V15

1. Desktop navigation is now a quiet vertical House index rather than a floating app rail.
2. The sealed Tonight object becomes real Programme Paper, creating stronger material contrast while keeping the title absent.
3. Trust Us uses large type as scenery around the central House axis.
4. The Room gains real perspective / Z-depth and a more dominant typographic environment.
5. Circle uses the most saturated brand field only for the act of sending.
6. Library becomes hold-and-drag horizontal typography, not a vertical CMS list.
7. Page change is led by the persistent House axis, which briefly widens and resolves into the next chapter.
8. Scroll speed adds only a very small amount of optical softness, keeping the reference to film transport restrained.

## What not to copy from award sites

- No gratuitous 3D object just because WebGL is fashionable.
- No long preloader.
- No custom cursor.
- No sound requirement.
- No scroll-jacking on Tonight.
- No motion that delays the core film ritual.
- No decorative film clichés.

The product promise still wins over spectacle.
