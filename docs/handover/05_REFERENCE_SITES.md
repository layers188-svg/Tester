# Motion and experience references

These are behaviour references, not design templates.

## Primary

### Siena Film Foundation

https://siena.film/

Study:

- continuous spatial environment
- drag / scrub feeling
- strong relationship between movement and film material
- navigation that feels like moving through one world

House Dark takeaway: the House should feel continuous. State changes should have direction, depth and persistence.

### Seasoned by Koto

https://seasoned.koto.studio/

Study:

- object transformations
- interface mode changes that feel material rather than routed

House Dark takeaway: sealed objects should become clue, playback or reveal states rather than being replaced.

### Mouthful of Dust

https://www.slv.vic.gov.au/mouthful-of-dust/

Study:

- direct manipulation
- environment changes around the user's action
- camera / viewpoint as part of storytelling

House Dark takeaway: hold, dim and seal interactions should cause the transformation themselves.

### PP Fragment

https://pp-fragment.com/

Study:

- typography as moving graphic material
- scale and space used as interaction

House Dark takeaway: The Room should be made of words, not comment cards.
