# House Dark design system

## Brand principle

The best film experiences happen when you know nothing.

House Dark is editorial, cinematic, private, calm and human. It should feel prepared carefully, not generated by a template.

## Approved identity

Use the exact files in `/brand_assets`:

- `House_Dark_Wordmark.svg`
- `House_Dark_Wordmark_Inverse.svg`
- `House_Dark_HD_Monogram_Approved.svg`
- `House_Dark_HD_Monogram_Approved_Inverse.svg`

Never rebuild the wordmark or monogram from font characters.

## Palette

Primary system:

- House Black `#080807`
- Projection Black `#030303`
- Programme / Stock Cream `#E9E0D0`
- Aged Paper `#D8CCB8`
- Oxblood `#65131F`
- Deep Burgundy `#741724`
- Patina Brass `#B9A16C`
- Tobacco `#956433`
- Cool Green `#36524C`
- Dust Rose `#A88187`

Principle:

Black is not pure. Paper is not white. Brass is not gold foil. Oxblood is not a red carpet.

Brass is for timing, hairlines and actions. It is not a background.

## Typography

Preferred production type system from the House Dark brand work:

- Newsreader for editorial statements, film titles after reveal and six-word responses
- Regular Barlow for paragraphs and form copy
- Barlow Condensed for navigation, labels, programme codes, timing, buttons and metadata

Do not include font files in this handover. Use the project's licensed / existing webfont setup or approved hosted source.

Do not set paragraphs in the condensed face.

## Layout

- Phone first and portrait first
- 16 to 20 px mobile gutters
- roughly 72 to 76 rem desktop site container
- narrative measures around 30 to 38 rem
- square edges and fine rules
- 44 px minimum touch targets
- strong vertical rhythm
- one dominant idea per screen

Desktop widens the phone hierarchy. It does not become a dashboard.

## Material fields

Use fields deliberately:

- Projection black for the room before the picture
- Programme paper for a reveal or programme moment
- Oxblood for one warm social / Circle moment
- House black for The Room

Avoid using every brand colour on every screen.

## Interface character

Use:

- opening numbers
- programme codes
- title-free abstract No Trailer material
- physical light
- restrained paper texture
- simple rules
- generous negative space
- controlled cropping

Avoid:

- generic rounded cards
- glass panels
- heavy shadows
- stock cinema interiors
- poster grids
- streaming rails
- neon
- fake film props
- decorative gradients without a physical light source
- generated people
- generic luxury-fashion language

## Copy tone

Calm. Specific. Literate. Human.

Confident enough to leave space.

Useful House Dark language:

- `Tonight is sealed.`
- `Find the joy in not knowing.`
- `See Tonight's Clue`
- `No Trailer`
- `Trust us.`
- `Seen it.`
- `That's all you get.`
- `Go in blind.`
- `Send Under Seal`
- `Sent Under Seal`
- `The Room is open.`
- `What stayed with you?`
- `Six words after the picture.`
- `Your reaction first.`

Avoid inflated tech language, AI language, hype, fake scarcity and obvious LLM phrasing.
